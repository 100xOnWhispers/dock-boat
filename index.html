Gunnery Trial

Learn the ship controls, manage limited ammo, and work through a random drill layout to sink the stationary target ship.

Controls

  • W / S or arrow up/down to control speed
  • A / D or arrow left/right to turn
  • Q / E fire port or starboard broadside

Mission

  • The target ship is anchored and will not move.
  • Rocks and reefs damage your hull, while buoys mark the safer lane.
  • Your ship starts with single-shot broadsides and limited ammo.
  • Touch your home island to reload and the upgrade island to unlock triple-shot volleys.

Press Enter to start or restart.

W / S or arrows sail
